# ARIA: Runtime Inspection and Amendment for Multi-Agent Systems

The site presents exactly two viewport-sized sections using one dark console
style. Purple identifies ARIA and intervention, red marks the original failure,
and green marks the successful saved outcome. Decorative counters, provenance
footers, and other small annotations are omitted from the main exhibit.

## Concept

ARIA is enabled by default and occupies a flat module bay above the team. Five
concept phases show Connect, Run, Inspect, Amend, and Resume. Inspect scans the
wrong decision at Review. Amend replaces that decision in the same slot. The
final delivery node and outcome show a green check. Removing ARIA hides its
module and connection and leaves the wrong decision with a red failed outcome.
Reconnecting starts a new cycle. The visible title and document title use the
full project name above.

## Benchmark canvas

Each selected benchmark automatically plays its archived story, then holds the
successful outcome. Only the active benchmark tab displays progress. The title
has pause/continue/replay, restart, and evidence controls. There is no separate
four-phase selector or progress display.

The trajectory preserves original event order. Playback follows each event,
then centers ARIA's Inspect location, identifying the responsible role and event.
The Inspect card explains the fault. The adjacent Amend card highlights the
reviewed original and amended decision. The final result presents the original
failure and saved successful continuation with a prominent green check.

The canvas has no internal vertical scrolling. The trajectory may scroll
horizontally to keep the active event visible. Desktop shows the decision pair
side by side. Narrow screens show the original during inspection and the amended
content during amendment and completion. Short phone screens omit the task
summary and completed trajectory strip while retaining the full task action,
Inspect identity, full reviewed amendment, and outcome. Full source messages,
complete amendments, benchmark tasks, evaluations, and limitations open in an
evidence dialog with its own reading surface.

## Playback and evidence

One animation clock drives visual changes and benchmark progress. Section and
browser visibility suspend playback without losing its position. Manual pause
and restart are available. The evidence dialog pauses playback. Reduced-motion
users see the completed view without autoplay.

Playback illustrates archived evidence; it does not execute models or rerun
experiments. UI labels use Inspect and Amend. Archived messages, code, metadata
keys, and evaluations retain their original text. Raw projections are unchanged.
